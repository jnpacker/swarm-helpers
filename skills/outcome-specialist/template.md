## Workflow Exit Criteria

| Status | Exit Criteria |
|---|---|
| **New** | Stakeholders identified, Product Manager identified, Architect identified, Assignee identified, success criteria documented, parent Strategic Goal linked (HATSTRAT), stack rank assigned |
| **Refinement** | Measurable metrics baselined, linked Features and Initiatives identified, timeline documented, quarterly review cadence established, roadmaps updated |
| **In Progress** | Underlying Features and Initiatives in active delivery; quarterly reviews being conducted; metrics being tracked |
| **Evaluation** | All underlying Features and Initiatives closed; post-completion review scheduled; actual results being measured against success criteria |
| **Closed** | Post-completion review complete, actual results documented, resolution value set |

## Scope Signals — Questions to Consider

> These are guidelines, not hard rules. Leadership may intentionally scope differently. Surface these as questions, not rejections.

- Does this Outcome describe a **measurable change in human behavior or business result**? If not, consider whether Components/Labels would be more appropriate.
- Is the scope limited to a **single product or single Release**? If so, confirm whether a Feature is a better fit — or document why Outcome-level framing is intentional.
- Are there **baselines and targets** for the metrics? Vague measurements weaken the grading.
- Does this Outcome have **cross-team or cross-product** implications, or is it entirely within one team's control?
