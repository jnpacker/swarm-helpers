## Workflow Exit Criteria

| Status | Exit Criteria |
|---|---|
| **New** | Stakeholders identified, Product Manager identified, Architect identified, Assignee identified, SMEs identified, goals documented, success criteria documented |
| **Refinement** | MVP described, risks and dependencies outlined, Epics produced, priority assigned, reviewed by all functions, roadmaps updated |
| **In Progress** | Success criteria met |
| **Review** | Stakeholders have confirmed whether success criteria was fully, partially, or not met |
| **Closed** | Resolution value set, all underlying Epics closed |

## Scope Signals — Questions to Consider

> These are guidelines, not hard rules. Leadership may intentionally scope differently. Surface these as questions, not rejections.

- Does this Initiative benefit **Red Hat associates** rather than customers directly? If customers are the primary beneficiary, a Feature may be a better fit.
- Is the work extending significantly beyond 6 months or a single Quarter/Release? Confirm whether splitting is appropriate or if the extended scope is intentional.
- Is there a clear success criterion that answers: "What can now move forward that couldn't before?" or "What is measurably better?"
